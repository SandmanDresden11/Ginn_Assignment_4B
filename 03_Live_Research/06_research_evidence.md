## Candidate sources

### 1. HubSpot — "2026 Entry and Tiers Policy for HubSpot Solutions Partners"
- **Publisher:** HubSpot (first-party, official policy page)
- **Publication/update date:** Effective July 15, 2026 (per page)
- **URL:** https://www.hubspot.com/2026-entry-and-tiers-policy-for-hubspot-solutions-partners
- **Factual claim:** As of July 15, 2026, the program has five tiers (Solutions Partner, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Elite). Entry requires the Partner SKU (or $400+/month software spend), a signed HSPPA, and Partner Certification; effective the same date, all partners must also purchase a Partner Program Membership. Tier point thresholds (to be met by January 15, 2026) run from Gold (110 sourced points/$2,200 MRR + 38 managed points/$3,800 MRR) up to Elite (2,100 sourced points/$42,000 MRR + 1,700 managed points/$170,000 MRR).
- **Meeting issue it connects to:** BluePeak wants "current public partner-program requirements and certification paths" before formalizing a co-selling relationship.
- **What it changes/confirms/deprioritizes:** Confirms concrete, current tier thresholds and surfaces a very recent change — a new Partner Program Membership purchase requirement effective July 15, 2026, just two weeks before this search. This should be verified with Simone before anyone assumes the older (pre-July 15) entry terms still apply, and gives Alana real revenue benchmarks to judge which tier is realistic for a 22-person firm.
- **Relevance score:** 3
- **Credibility/recency note:** First-party, official, and precisely dated — very high credibility and unusually current for a policy page.
- **Human-review flag:** Pricing (partner terms)

### 2. HubSpot Academy — "Academy certifications | Frequently Asked Questions"
- **Publisher:** HubSpot (first-party Academy documentation)
- **Publication/update date:** Not explicitly dated (current live FAQ)
- **URL:** https://knowledge.hubspot.com/help-and-resources/hubspot-academy-certification-frequently-asked-questions
- **Factual claim:** The Partner Certification is an 11-part course (10 classes, ~5 hours of instruction) plus a 90-minute exam, covering the Market/Sell/Deliver/Grow methodology. Only one person in an organization needs to pass it for the organization to become a certified Solutions Partner; publishing a Partner Directory profile requires at least one certification, and maintaining tiered status requires passing and maintaining the Partner Certification.
- **Meeting issue it connects to:** Alana's stated concern about "delivery capacity and certification time" before committing to larger engagements.
- **What it changes/confirms/deprioritizes:** Good news for near-term planning: baseline certification is a modest time investment (~6.5 hours for one person), which should ease Alana's certification-timeline worry specifically. The larger open question — delivery capacity against the tier point thresholds in source 1 — is not resolved by this source and remains for Alana to size separately.
- **Relevance score:** 3
- **Credibility/recency note:** First-party HubSpot Academy documentation — high credibility.
- **Human-review flag:** None

### 3. HubSpot — "HubSpot Sales Partner Program: Referral Partners / Solutions Partners" (PDF, hosted on HubSpot's CDN)
- **Publisher:** HubSpot (first-party, hosted on hubfs/cdn2.hubspot.net)
- **Publication/update date:** Not explicitly dated in search result; confirm current version before relying on it
- **URL:** https://cdn2.hubspot.net/hubfs/53/SalesSolutionsPartnerPDFs.pdf
- **Factual claim:** Partner economics include a recurring software margin (roughly 20% of first-year subscription revenue at base tier, with enhanced economics at higher tiers) and a 20% commission for 3 years on partner-sourced deals. A separate Upmarket Referral Program pays 20% commission for 1 year on deals co-sold with HubSpot's own direct sales team, but that program is restricted to Platinum/Diamond/Elite tier partners with CRM Implementation accreditation, on Partner-Assisted deals of at least $3,000 MRR selling Professional or Enterprise subscriptions.
- **Meeting issue it connects to:** Simone's caution that she "avoided promising leads, margins, or program benefits without confirming the current terms," and the group's plan to test two pilot opportunities with any commercial arrangement "subject to current program rules."
- **What it changes/confirms/deprioritizes:** Confirms the base commission structure, but also shows that the richer co-sell economics Theo may be hoping for require Platinum tier or above plus a specific accreditation — likely out of reach for BluePeak at initial entry. This should directly inform the "qualification criteria for two suitable opportunities" that Maya and Theo are defining, so expectations are set at the base-tier commission level, not the upmarket one.
- **Relevance score:** 3
- **Credibility/recency note:** First-party document hosted on HubSpot's own file system — high credibility, though the exact publish/update date wasn't visible in the search result; confirm it is the current version (not a prior year's PDF) before using it with BluePeak.
- **Human-review flag:** Pricing (partner terms)
