## Candidate sources

### 1. HubSpot — "Revenue Hub" pricing page
- **Publisher:** HubSpot (first-party pricing page)
- **Publication/update date:** Current live page as of 2026-07-28
- **URL:** https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/revenue
- **Factual claim:** Standard Revenue Hub pricing is listed at $95/user/month (Professional) and $140/user/month (Enterprise); a promotional rate of $57/mo/seat (Professional) and $98/mo/seat (Enterprise) is currently available for new Sales Hub + Revenue Hub customers on annual billing. Commerce Hub was retired and rebranded as Revenue Hub in June 2026. Billing and payments remain free through August 2026, with a new pricing model taking effect in September 2026.
- **Meeting issue it connects to:** Devon's detailed pricing questions and Alex's stated need to "verify current packaging and pricing before presenting a proposal because public offerings can change."
- **What it changes/confirms/deprioritizes:** This is time-sensitive: HubSpot's own page confirms a billing/payments pricing change is scheduled for September 2026 — about six weeks from today. Any proposal built on current promotional pricing should explicitly flag this upcoming change to Devon rather than presenting today's numbers as fixed. Directly shapes Alex's "current, clearly dated packaging summary" deliverable.
- **Relevance score:** 3
- **Credibility/recency note:** First-party pricing page — highest credibility and current, but pricing pages change without notice; confirm figures again immediately before the proposal is sent.
- **Human-review flag:** Pricing

### 2. HubSpot — "Introducing Revenue Hub: Quote to cash, finally in one place"
- **Publisher:** HubSpot (first-party company news)
- **Publication/update date:** 2026 (tied to Revenue Hub launch)
- **URL:** https://www.hubspot.com/company-news/introducing-revenue-hub-quote-to-cash-finally-in-one-place
- **Factual claim:** Revenue Hub collects payments via native HubSpot Payments or a connected Stripe account, and supports payment links, invoice/quote payments, subscription billing, stored payment methods, and AI-prioritized (Breeze Assistant) overdue-invoice reminders; native processing is available in the US, UK, and Canada, with Stripe covering global processing.
- **Meeting issue it connects to:** Devon's question about "whether it could reduce manual quoting and payment follow-up," and BrightPath's existing use of Stripe for payments.
- **What it changes/confirms/deprioritizes:** Confirms Revenue Hub can connect to BrightPath's existing Stripe account rather than requiring a payment-processor switch, and automates overdue-invoice follow-up — directly answers Devon's core question and should anchor the "proposal-to-payment" workflow in Maya's pilot scorecard.
- **Relevance score:** 3
- **Credibility/recency note:** First-party, high credibility.
- **Human-review flag:** None

### 3. Aggregated Zoho pricing guides — "Zoho One Pricing 2026" (Capterra listing, Zenatta Consulting, and similar third-party pricing guides)
- **Publisher:** Third-party pricing-guide sites (not Zoho's own pricing page, which did not appear directly in results)
- **Publication/update date:** 2026
- **URL:** https://www.capterra.com/p/166175/Zoho-One/ (representative; corroborated by https://zenatta.com/zoho-pricing-guide-2025/ and https://zohobyc2m.com/zoho-one-pricing-2026-simple-guide-for-businesses-plans/)
- **Factual claim:** Zoho One offers two pricing models: a "Flexible User" plan (~$90/user/month, annual) that licenses only active users, and an "All Employee" plan (~$37/user/month, annual) that requires licensing the company's entire headcount, not just CRM users.
- **Meeting issue it connects to:** Devon's belief that Zoho One "may be cheaper," which the team has not yet tested for reporting or implementation effort.
- **What it changes/confirms/deprioritizes:** Complicates Devon's assumption: the lower $37/user/month figure only applies if BrightPath licenses its **entire** staff, not just the sales/marketing/support users who would touch a CRM. If most employees don't need CRM access, the cheaper-looking "All Employee" plan could cost more in aggregate than HubSpot's per-seat pricing. This nuance should be built into the total-operating-effort comparison the group agreed to run.
- **Relevance score:** 3
- **Credibility/recency note:** Third-party pricing-guide content, not Zoho's own pricing page — treat as directional and confirm exact current figures directly on Zoho's site before quoting to BrightPath.
- **Human-review flag:** Pricing

### 4. HubSpot Knowledge Base — "How to Understand Attribution Report Definitions in HubSpot's Report Builder"
- **Publisher:** HubSpot (first-party product documentation)
- **Publication/update date:** Not explicitly dated (current live article)
- **URL:** https://knowledge.hubspot.com/reports/understand-attribution-reporting
- **Factual claim:** Contacts who register for or attend a webinar (tracked via the Marketing Events/Campaign object) receive attribution credit if later associated with a closed-won deal; HubSpot's Marketing Campaigns 2.0 tool supports multi-touch attribution and can regroup revenue-attribution reports by Content Title or Landing Page rather than just Channel. Third-party webinar platforms (Zoom, Hopin, ON24) integrate via form-submission or meeting-registration events.
- **Meeting issue it connects to:** Reese's stated goal of "cleaner attribution from webinar registration through contract renewal."
- **What it changes/confirms/deprioritizes:** Confirms native attribution from webinar registration through to closed-won revenue is supported today. It does **not** explicitly confirm attribution continuing through renewal/expansion revenue specifically — that distinction should be verified in-product before promising "registration-to-renewal" attribution as a complete pilot deliverable.
- **Relevance score:** 3
- **Credibility/recency note:** First-party documentation — high credibility for the acquisition-to-close portion of the claim; the renewal-tracking extension is not fully confirmed by this source.
- **Human-review flag:** None
